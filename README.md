# GMTK26
Our game for GMTK Game Jam 2026

## Idea:

There's bombs at yo house, 4 of them, connected somehow, that ypu have to diffuse them in specific order. The mechanics are all user speed based, the user has to find the shortest routes to places, avoid moving threats which have their own routes which eventually affects the players route. In a specific order the player has to perform actions by interaction, the player will have the ability to move 2 items at a time.
To make the experience intense, there will be no timer. Wait what? Then how's it a countdown?
What the player hears is the sound of ticking bombs tick faster gradually, plus they can go to a bomb to see its exact time. 
The game will not guide the player what to do, hence level design has to be perfect to give the right direction, without giving a curry of steps on what to do which makes it all boring
The bomb will be on a 6 minute 42 second  timer, but the level has to be played multiple times to get urself able to do it in time, hence giving a playtime of at least 30 minutes
